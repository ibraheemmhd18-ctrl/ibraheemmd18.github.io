⚡ Welcome to mBlock Arduino IDE!
📌 HOW TO USE:
1️⃣ Look at the LEFT sidebar → categories: Pins, Analog, Control, Serial
2️⃣ CLICK on any block and DRAG it into the white area
3️⃣ Connect blocks vertically (they snap together)
4️⃣ Click "Compile & Download" to get Arduino code
5️⃣ Upload the .ino file using Arduino IDE or arduino-cli
✅ Drag a "pin mode" block and "digital write" to start!
